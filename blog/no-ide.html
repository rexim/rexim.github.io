No IDE

You can't develop anything serious without an IDE in 2025. But what can you develop without an IDE? Certainly you can write a "Hello, World" in Python without an IDE. It's just a single line of code afer all. You can do it in a notepad.

print("Hello, World")

You can probably even disable syntax highlighting for this one and imagine that you are a hardcore low-level dev from 70s.

But does anything slightly more complicated than that require an IDE? Well the next program by complexity after "Hello, World" is "What is your name?"

name = input("What is your name? ") print(f"Hello {name}!")

This one employs not only output, but also an input, and even a variable assignment! A rather complex interactive program. Still quite easy to whip out off the top of your head in a notepad.

The question is if you keep raising complexity of smoothly at which point you simply can't develop without an IDE? I suppose for different people it's going to be a differen point
